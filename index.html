      HOW TO PLAY

      🎯 Objective

      Click the "Here!" button as fast as you can! Avoid clicking "Not Here!" decoy buttons which penalize your timer.

      ⚡ Game Modes

      Timer Mode: Fast arcade mode where time limit shrinks each round.

      Zen Mode: Relaxed mode with no clock.

      ⭐ EXP & Leveling Rules

      ⚡ +1 EXP for every button clicked!

      🏆 +10 BONUS EXP whenever you set a new High Score! 🎉

      🔓 High Score = Max Level Cap! Keep pushing higher scores in Timer or Zen to break your level cap & level up!
